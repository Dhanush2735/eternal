# The final test in the real game (step 60)

*This is the session script for the run the sandbox cannot make: a real Terra++ world, the game's
picture, true multiplayer. Until a session reports here, nothing is claimed as passed. What the
sandbox already proved is part P; everything else is yours, and its results are step 61's input.*

55 tests in 8 phases. Every test names what good looks like and where it comes from. **No place or
year comes first** (turn 96): walk the route in any order, but walk all of it.

## P - pre-flight: the sandbox must already be green

1. **P1** (the sandbox, before the session): run `worldfill/sweep_all.sh`, the syntax gate and the
   manifest build; rebuild the mod. Good: every probe is green (101 as shipped), no syntax errors,
   the manifest matches the data. *From: turn 91's rule; the sweep; step 26's manifest.*
2. **P2** (your server, before the session): `/ee check all` on the server, as console and as a
   player. Good: every check passes or names a known limit; no file is created in the world folder.
   *From: the `TEST_CHECKLIST.md` tradition.*

## G - guardrails: the rules the game must hold

46. **G1** (two players, any year): ask the date from two players in different places. Good: one
    shared date and one timeline: both see the same day; different years means different universes,
    never one world. *From: decision 3.*
47. **G2** (any place, **2100 and 2500**): ask who rules, who lives here, what things cost and what
    happened. Good: every answer past 2026 carries the projected mark; no ruler, head of government,
    person, dynasty, war, event, disaster or price exists; no population figure past the last
    measured year. *From: the AD 3000 guardrail (turn 110).*
48. **G3** (the purse, any year): try to spend more than you have. Good: the purse cannot go below
    zero: the trade refuses and says why. *From: step 23a's rule.*
49. **G4** (the timeline, two universes): write a note, branch a timeline, then travel both ways.
    Good: notes are written once; a branch is a difference from its parent; nobody stands in two
    universes at once; the way back is booked before the way out. *From: steps 51-56.*
50. **G5** (a fixed point, its year): try to change a fixed point, then rebuild it. Good: it refuses
    and names the safe way out; no dice; forcing breaks the point and the break is handed over with
    its why; a player is never erased. *From: steps 53 and 56; decision 7 policy OFF.*
51. **G6** (a night outside town, any year): wait outside a town at night. Good: no monsters, no
    raids, no wandering traders in history. *From: known problem 9; verify the step 21 fix stays.*

## What to write down when the run is over

One page per phase: what you did, what you saw, what failed. Passes are cheap; **a failure wants the
place, the year, the exact command and what the screen showed** - that is step 61's whole input.
Known limits (problems 73 and 81 in the route, the thin name lists, the estimates marked estimates)
are not failures: they are the documented limits, and the test is that the game **says so honestly**
instead of inventing.
