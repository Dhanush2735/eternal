# Phase 14: Era-Morphing Architecture & 53-Carto Infrastructure Synthesis

## 1. Overview & Vision
Phase 14 delivers an authentic historical transformation of the Minecraft Earth world. Instead of modern asphalt highways and power lines existing in 500 BCE, or static 7×7 box buildings appearing everywhere regardless of culture, Eternal Engine now dynamically morphs roads, utilities, and architecture across epochs:
- **53-Carto Infrastructure Synthesis**: Commands Terra++'s linear and polygonal infrastructure generators to suppress anachronistic structures (e.g. power lines before 1882, railways before 1825) and adapt roadways and lighting to historical periods.
- **Procedural Architectural Grammars**: Replaces generic boxes with authentic cultural architectural styles (Classical Greco-Roman, Medieval Gothic, East Asian Pagoda, Islamic Saracenic, Industrial Victorian, Modern Metropolitan).
- **Historical Landmark Generation**: Procedurally constructs monumental civic architecture (Parthenon, Roman Colosseum, Medieval Fortress Keep, Great Pagoda, Gothic Cathedral) at surface elevations.
- **Atomic Temporal Rebuilding**: Manages glitch-free temporal jumps via `TemporalMorphController`, ordering top-to-bottom demolition and bottom-to-top reconstruction through the asynchronous `BlockUpdateQueue`.

## 2. Core Architectural Components

### A. 53-Carto Infrastructure Synthesis (`TerraTimeBridge.java`)
Regulates the chronological validity of modern infrastructure elements generated by Terra++:
- **Technological Cutoffs (`isInfrastructureAllowed`)**:
  - `POWER_LINE`, `POWER_TOWER`, `PYLON`: Suppressed before **1882** (Pearl Street station).
  - `RAILWAY`, `LIGHT_RAIL`, `SUBWAY`: Suppressed before **1825** (Stockton & Darlington).
  - `AERIALWAY`, `CABLE_CAR`: Suppressed before **1900**.
  - `RUNWAY`, `APRON`, `TAXIWAY`: Suppressed before **1910**.
  - `MOTORWAY`, `FREEWAY`: Suppressed before **1924** (Autostrada dei Laghi).
  - `PIPELINE`: Suppressed before **1865**.
- **Road Surface Morphing (`getRoadBlockForEra`)**:
  - *Ancient (<500 CE)*: Major highways use Roman stone pavers (`COBBLESTONE`), minor roads use `DIRT_PATH` / `COARSE_DIRT`.
  - *Medieval & Early Modern (500–1850 CE)*: Cobblestone pavers and packed mud.
  - *Industrial (1850–1925 CE)*: Belgian paving blocks (`STONE_BRICKS`) and macadam gravel.
  - *Modern (>1925 CE)*: Asphalt concrete (`GRAY_CONCRETE`) and smooth stone curbs.
- **Street Illumination (`getLampBlockForEra`)**:
  - Pre-1880: Iron lanterns and torches (`LANTERN`).
  - Post-1880: Electric and gas illumination (`SEA_LANTERN`).

### B. Procedural Architectural Grammar (`CivilizationGrammarEngine.java`)
Constructs authentic structures following formal architectural rules:
1. **Cultural Archetypes**:
   - `CLASSICAL_GRECO_ROMAN`: Quartz pillar colonnades, smooth sandstone stylobate, low-pitch terracotta pediment.
   - `MEDIEVAL_GOTHIC`: Stone brick buttresses, pointed arches, spruce wood gables, stained glass/iron bars.
   - `EAST_ASIAN_PAGODA`: Dark oak post-and-beam frame, red terracotta infill, dark prismarine flared eaves, dougong brackets.
   - `ISLAMIC_SARACENIC`: Smooth sandstone walls, chiseled trim, glazed terracotta floor, gold dome crowns.
   - `INDUSTRIAL_VICTORIAN`: Red brick masonry, cast iron framework, multi-pane factory windows, deepslate tile roofs.
   - `MODERN_METROPOLITAN`: Smooth concrete, tinted glass curtain walls, steel framework, flat utility roofs.
2. **Decomposition & Construction Sequence**:
   - Foundation plinth & floor step.
   - Rhythmic perimeter colonnade & pilaster bays (spaced every 3 blocks).
   - Infill walls with recessed window apertures and entry arches.
   - Projecting cornice and entablature trim.
   - Authentic culture-specific roof geometry.

### C. Historical Landmark Generator (`HistoricalLandmarkGenerator.java`)
Procedurally generates iconic monuments and civic structures:
- **Parthenon**: Octastyle peripteral temple (17×31 blocks) with quartz peristyle columns, interior cella chamber, and terracotta pediment gable.
- **Medieval Fortress Keep**: 15×15 stone brick keep with 4 corner watchtowers, arrow slits, and crenelated parapets.
- **Great Pagoda**: 5-tiered East Asian pagoda with dark oak frame, red terracotta walls, flared eaves, and a gold spire.
- **Roman Colosseum**: Elliptical double-radius arcade tiers with sandstone colonnades and internal arena space.
- **Gothic Cathedral**: 26-block cruciform nave with stone buttress ribs, stained glass lancet windows, and pointed vaulted roofing.
- *Strict Elevation Constraint*: All landmark blocks are validated above surface ground $Y$ (`TerraCoordinateHelper.getSurfaceY`) to preserve terrain integrity.

### D. Atomic Temporal Rebuilding (`TemporalMorphController.java`)
Coordinates temporal transitions:
- Freezes simulation chunk ticking during the transition window (20 ticks).
- Orders top-to-bottom demolition of obsolete structures (`scheduleDemolition`) via `BlockUpdateQueue` to avoid floating artifacts.
- Synchronizes the Terra++ generator and time index via `TerraTimeBridge.jumpToYear`.
- Enqueues bottom-to-top reconstruction (`scheduleReconstruction`) for period replacements.

## 3. In-Game Commands (`/ee morph ...`)

| Command | Permission | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `/ee morph status` | All Players | Displays temporal morph controller status (freeze status, total morphs, last morphed year). |
| `/ee morph jump <year>` | OP Level 2 | Triggers an atomic temporal transition to the specified year (e.g. -500, 1200, 1880, 2026). |
| `/ee morph landmark <type>` | OP Level 2 | Procedurally constructs a historical landmark (`PARTHENON`, `MEDIEVAL_KEEP`, `GREAT_PAGODA`, `ROMAN_COLOSSEUM`, `GOTHIC_CATHEDRAL`) at player coordinates. |
| `/ee morph grammar <culture> <type>` | OP Level 2 | Generates authentic architecture (`CLASSICAL_GRECO_ROMAN`, `MEDIEVAL_GOTHIC`, `EAST_ASIAN_PAGODA`, `ISLAMIC_SARACENIC`, `INDUSTRIAL_VICTORIAN`, `MODERN_METROPOLITAN`). |
